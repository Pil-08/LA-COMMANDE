Cream, ink and gold

Nikkei

A cover of changing photos with a line over each, a fine serif with one word in italics and sections alternating cream and ink. The menu is text only, centred; below, a ribbon of photos, opening hours with “open now” and booking one tap away. It asks for good photos: this is the style that shows them off best.

Who it suits
Chef-led restaurants, bars with a cocktail list and venues with good photography.
Type and colour
Cormorant Garamond and Hanken Grotesk · cream, ink and one gold.
